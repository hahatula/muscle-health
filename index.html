Frequently asked questions

A closer look.

What is MeNow offering?

A patent-pending portfolio of bioactive compound applications with potential relevance to muscle preservation, available for licensing and application-specific development.

Does MeNow supply the compounds?

MeNow licenses intellectual property and ingredient applications rather than selling ingredients. A technical meeting can clarify sourcing considerations for the candidates relevant to your project.

Which products could this portfolio support?

Potential areas include healthy-aging nutrition, weight management, sports nutrition and recovery-focused products. Candidate selection and formulation depend on the intended audience, format and market.

How does this relate to GLP-1 weight management?

Muscle preservation is a potential product-development focus alongside GLP-1-led weight management. This portfolio concerns muscle-health applications; it is separate from MeNow’s FitMeNow™ satiety opportunity.

What evidence is available for the compounds?

MeNow can review the scientific rationale and available candidate-specific information with prospective partners, then identify the evaluation needed for the intended application.

What does the food-use foundation mean for formulation?

Existing food uses and sourcing can provide a practical starting point. The selected compound, formulation, use level and target market determine the requirements for a new application.

What can be included in a license?

The relevant compound applications and associated intellectual property are discussed with each partner. Application scope, territory, rights and commercial terms are defined in the licensing agreement.

How can we get started?

Bring your target audience, product format and market to an introductory meeting. Together we can explore candidate fit, technical information and next steps for evaluation and licensing.
